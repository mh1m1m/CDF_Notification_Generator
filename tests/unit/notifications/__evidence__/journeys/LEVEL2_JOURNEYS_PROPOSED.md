Every notification card (generator «مسار الحالة» and committee register cards) will show three nested levels:

1. **Level 1 — the case's own journey** (master procedure: 5 stages S1–S5 and 28 sub-states, from the V8 workbook). Identical for every notification; the card marks where the case stands when the notice is sent.
2. **Level 2 — the notification's purpose journey**: five stages worded for the notification's object and the recipient's capacity. Stage 5 names what closes for that recipient; it never says the case is closed.
3. **Level 3 — the steps inside the current level-2 stage**, with the step this notice reports.

## 4. How the three levels will appear on a card

Level 1 (top): the five master stages with the case's position (✔ passed · ● current · ○ ahead) and the current sub-state caption for internal recipients. Level 2 (middle): the five stages of the recipient's purpose journey, with the stage this notice belongs to. Level 3 (bottom): the steps of that level-2 stage, with «this notice» on the step reported; alternative outcomes (returned, rejected, cancelled) appear only when they occur. Public and external recipients get the public labels only.
